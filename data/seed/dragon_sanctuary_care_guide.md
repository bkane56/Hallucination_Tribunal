# Dragon Sanctuary Care Guide

## Feeding Rules

Dragons must be fed twice daily with approved protein sources. Feeding after midnight is strictly prohibited due to metabolic instability risks documented in Section 4.2.

## Fireproofing Requirements

All enclosures must maintain Class-A fireproofing certification renewed annually.

## Visitor Restrictions

Visitors under 16 are not permitted in active dragon habitats without certified handler escort.

## Emergency Procedures

In case of dragon escape, activate containment protocol DRG-7 and notify sanctuary warden immediately.
