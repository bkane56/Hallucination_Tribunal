# Incident Response Runbook

## Reporting Timeline

Security incidents must be reported within 4 hours of discovery to the Security Operations Center.

Critical incidents affecting customer data require immediate escalation to the CISO.

## Escalation Matrix

- Severity 1: CISO, Legal, CEO notification within 1 hour.
- Severity 2: Security team lead within 2 hours.
- Severity 3: Ticket queue within 8 hours.

## Required Steps

1. Contain the incident.
2. Preserve evidence and logs.
3. Notify stakeholders per escalation matrix.
4. Document timeline and impact assessment.
5. Conduct post-incident review within 5 business days.

## AI-Specific Incidents

Incidents involving AI model hallucinations affecting customer-facing decisions require immediate human review and model rollback if accuracy cannot be verified.
