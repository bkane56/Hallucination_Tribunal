# Data Privacy and Classification Policy

## Classification Levels

1. Public - May be shared externally.
2. Internal - For employees only.
3. Confidential - Requires need-to-know access.
4. Restricted - Includes PHI, PII, and regulated data.

## PII and PHI Handling

Personally identifiable information and protected health information must never be pasted into public chatbots or unapproved AI tools.

PHI may not be processed by hosted AI models without a signed BAA and explicit policy exception.

## Customer Data

Customer data classified as confidential or restricted requires encryption in transit and at rest. External AI processing requires legal review.

## Incident Reporting

Security incidents involving data exposure must be reported within 4 hours to security@company.internal.

## SOC 2

This policy supports SOC 2 Type II controls for data handling and access management.
